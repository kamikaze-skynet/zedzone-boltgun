Makes .75 bolt weapons behave like real self-propelled explosive rounds.

[b]What a round does[/b]
[list]
[*] A propellant charge launches it out of the barrel at 300 m/s
[*] A rocket motor lights and boosts it to 975 m/s
[*] The hardened tip punches through armour and hits like a 20 mm autocannon round
[*] A split second later the payload detonates inside the target, followed by a small blast
[/list]

Works with the .75 bolt weapon packs (Workshop items 3815200763 and 3815190597). You need those installed.
This mod adds no items or art. It only changes how the rounds fly and hit.

Every number (speeds, damage, fuse delay, blast size) can be changed in BepInEx\config\girthy_mods.zedzone.massreactive.cfg.

Needs BepInEx (IL2CPP). Log: BepInEx\MassReactive.log
