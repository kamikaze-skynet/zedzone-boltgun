A .75 rocket-bolt rifle and pistol for ZED ZONE.

[b]Adds[/b]
[list]
[*] Boltgun - full-auto .75 rifle, 30-round magazine
[*] Bolt Pistol - one-handed .75 sidearm, 10-round magazine
[*] .75 Bolt ammo and both magazines (all craftable, the guns also turn up rarely in buildings)
[/list]

[b]Every bolt is a small rocket[/b]
[list]
[*] A powder charge launches it out of the barrel at 300 m/s
[*] A rocket motor lights and boosts it to 975 m/s
[*] The fuse arms after 2 m of flight
[*] The hardened tip punches through armour and hits like a 20 mm autocannon round
[*] A split second later the charge detonates inside the target, followed by a small blast
[/list]

[b]Lore touches[/b]
[list]
[*] Every round is drawn as a bolt with a glowing rocket motor
[*] The motor lights with a flash, a puff of smoke and a hiss a few metres from the barrel, then leaves a flame and smoke trail
[*] Spent casings fly from the gun on every shot
[*] The internal charge throws shrapnel at anyone standing close to the target
[*] Anything that survives the charge is knocked off its feet
[*] The internal charge tears limbs off zombies using the game's own dismemberment
[*] Not every fuse is perfect: about 3 in 100 bolts are duds that only do the kinetic hit
[*] Bolts that strike a wall or the ground at a shallow angle glance off without going off
[*] Heavy recoil, worst on the one-handed Bolt Pistol
[/list]

In co-op, other players see your bolts fly and explode the same way. Everyone needs the mod.

Every number (speeds, damage, fuse delay, blast size) can be changed in BepInEx\config\girthy_mods.zedzone.boltgun.cfg.

Needs BepInEx (IL2CPP) with the ZED ZONE mod loader. Log: BepInEx\Boltgun.log
