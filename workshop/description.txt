A .75 rocket-bolt rifle and pistol for ZED ZONE.

[b]Adds[/b]
[list]
[*] Boltgun - full-auto .75 rifle, 30-round magazine
[*] Bolt Pistol - one-handed .75 sidearm, 10-round magazine
[*] .75 Bolt ammo and both magazines (all craftable, the guns also turn up rarely in buildings)
[/list]

[b]Every bolt is a small rocket[/b]
[list]
[*] A powder charge launches it out of the barrel at 300 m/s
[*] A rocket motor lights and boosts it to 975 m/s
[*] The fuse arms after 2 m of flight
[*] The hardened tip punches through armour and hits like a 20 mm autocannon round
[*] A split second later the charge detonates inside the target, followed by a small blast
[/list]

Every number (speeds, damage, fuse delay, blast size) can be changed in BepInEx\config\girthy_mods.zedzone.boltgun.cfg.

Needs BepInEx (IL2CPP) with the ZED ZONE mod loader. Log: BepInEx\Boltgun.log
